With this idea, I think I could arrange piecewise functions to play familiar tunes, or just see what a really complex function sounds like, however horrible that is. That is where the "Sing My Function" title comes in. This is exaclty what I'm envisioning: I input a function, or a piecewise, my program will display this on a beautiful plot using matplotlib, and, shortly after, it will sing the frequency, per time, at a set speed, and something interesting will be played. I want to mess around with the harmony and attack paramters as well to make the overall product sound better and be more versatile as well.
